Our story

A slower kind
of coffee.

SOSA began with a simple idea: coffee should be a daily ritual worth pausing for. From the first bean to the final drop, we believe the difference lives in the smallest details.

Our small roastery in the heart of London blends years of experience with hands-on craft, serving specialty coffee in a warm, considered space made for your own quiet moment.

Our craft

From bean
to cup.

Good coffee is not an accident. It is a series of small, deliberate decisions — from the farm gate to the final pour.

01

Sourcing

We work directly with growers, selecting seasonal beans and roast profiles that honour their origin and character.

02

Small-batch roasting

Every batch is roasted by hand in small quantities, protecting the balance of flavour and the freshness of the bean.

03

The brew

Our baristas dial in every detail — temperature, dose, and time — for a cup that is balanced every single time.
